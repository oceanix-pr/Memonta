Memonta 9.6.6（150）

- 将媒体导入、录音合并与媒体清理的进度提示统一显示在窗口顶部横幅，减少底部提示遮挡。
- 录音合并时继续显示处理阶段与进度；回声消除阶段仍可取消，取消后保留原始音轨。

此版本沿用未公证分发方式。首次安装可能被 macOS 阻止，更新后可能需要重新授予系统权限。

English

- Shows media import, recording merge, and media cleanup progress in the top banner to reduce obstruction at the bottom of the window.
- Keeps the merge stage and progress visible. Echo reduction can still be cancelled, falling back to the original audio track.

This release remains non-notarized. macOS may block first launch, and related system permissions may need to be granted again after updating.
