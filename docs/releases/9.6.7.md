Memonta 9.6.7（151）

- 在转写内容旁提示核对重要信息。
- 在 AI 生成的录音总结、图片笔记总结、画面要点和待办文档旁显示核实提示；手动创建的待办文档不显示该提示。
- 为上述提示补齐应用支持语言的翻译与无障碍组合朗读。

此版本沿用未公证分发方式。首次安装可能被 macOS 阻止，更新后可能需要重新授予系统权限。

English

- Adds a reminder to check important transcript content.
- Shows a verification reminder beside AI-generated recording and image-note summaries, visual notes, and to-do documents. Manually created to-do documents are excluded.
- Localizes the reminders for supported languages and combines the AI reminder for accessibility reading.

This release remains non-notarized. macOS may block first launch, and related system permissions may need to be granted again after updating.
