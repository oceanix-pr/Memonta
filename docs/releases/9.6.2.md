Memonta 9.6.2（146）

- 专业模式新增「清理录音」「清理图片」：删除媒体原件以释放空间，保留条目、转写、OCR、总结与待办。
- 右键菜单支持录音、图片、视频的单选和多选清理；混选时按媒体类型处理，并确认数量、预计体积与不可撤销的后果。
- 清理后保留图片与录屏类型，磁盘重建可恢复无原件的条目；清理视频继续要求可复用关键帧。
- 内容和元数据保存成功后才清理原件；批量部分失败明确反馈，清理与处理任务、磁盘同步和更新安装互斥。

此版本沿用未公证分发方式。临时签名不能保证跨版本保留 macOS 授权；系统要求时需要重新授权。

English

- Adds Delete Audio and Delete Images in Pro mode to free disk space while preserving entries, transcripts, OCR, summaries and to-dos.
- Adds single-selection and multiple-selection audio, image and video cleanup to context menus, with file counts, estimated space and irreversible consequences shown before confirmation.
- Preserves image and video entry types and restores entries without original media when rebuilding from disk. Video cleanup still requires reusable keyframes.
- Saves content and metadata before deleting originals, reports partial batch failures, and prevents cleanup from conflicting with processing, disk sync or update installation.

This release remains non-notarized. Ad-hoc signing cannot guarantee macOS authorization continuity across versions; reauthorization may be required.
